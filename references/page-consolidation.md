# Page consolidation and information density

Run this decision before Scene authoring. The unit of a management report is a complete management conclusion, not an isolated source paragraph, content atom, chart, risk note, or expression role.

## Consolidation order

For every adjacent candidate Scene, test in this order:

1. Define the overarching management question in business language. `evidence`, `risk`, `action`, `contrast`, and `mechanism` are expression roles, not management questions.
2. Remove the candidate Scene title mentally. If its content naturally becomes a module of the previous Scene, merge it.
3. Merge when the candidates jointly explain one business chain, causal chain, funnel, strategy system, operating action, or decision.
4. Preserve every source unit by hierarchy: primary answer, proof object, compact support module, decision-critical qualifier, or expandable detail. Never delete a sourced fact merely to reduce pages.
5. Split only when there is a genuinely independent management decision, a new story stage, incompatible visual semantics, or a verified 16:9 readability/capacity conflict.

An outline item recommends one integrated Scene. It may use a second Scene only for an independent decision or verified capacity conflict. Page numbering and outline labels never create Scenes. The renderer may not add a Scene beyond the plan without rerunning consolidation.

## Default density

Use management-report density by default, not keynote-style sparse density. One Scene may contain one core conclusion, three to six first-level modules, and eight to fifteen lightweight information units when the relationships remain readable. Multiple charts, numbers, steps, or submodules do not by themselves require a new Scene.

Prefer an integrated composition selected from the actual relationships: KPI strip plus funnel, upper/lower regions, left/right paths, matrix, process with supporting evidence, or strategy map. These are composition options, not mandatory templates. Visually demote supporting explanations instead of giving them a separate page.

For tables, size the table surface to its rendered rows; do not stretch a short table to fill the stage. Use the remaining space for a sourced interpretation, comparison, risk qualifier, or related module only when it belongs to the same management question. Do not add decorative filler.

## Whitespace acceptance

Whitespace must separate hierarchy, not substitute for missing composition. Balanced and compact Scenes must not contain a large unexplained empty band. A focused opening or section divider may be sparse; an evidence, mechanism, risk, action, table, or dashboard Scene may not.

Before approval, check the maximum continuous empty vertical band and the difference between a table container and the actual table height. Repair by consolidating related modules, changing composition, reducing unnecessary fixed heights, or using `align-self:start`. Do not repair by inventing content or shrinking body text below the contract.
